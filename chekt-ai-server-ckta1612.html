CHeKT AI Server: CKTAI612What Is the CHeKT AI Server CKTAI612?

The CHeKT AI Server (CKTAI612) is an AI video analytics appliance that adds twelve channels of deep learning analyticsdetection to the IP cameras, encoders, and recorders already on site — no full system replacement required, with no full system replacement. It is built on NVIDIA Jetson hardware, is NDAA and TAA compliant, and either turns detections into verified video alarms through a CHeKT Video Control Panel or streams analytics to an existing VMS or NVR over ONVIF.

Paired with a CHeKT Video Control Panel, every detection becomes a verified video alarm inside the monitoring center you already use.

What happens after detection?

Five steps from an installed camera to a dispatched response. 

Most AI appliances are built to finish at step two. CHeKT is built for what happens next.Detection happens at step two. The CKTAI612 is designed to carry it through steps three to five.

STEP ONE: CAMERAS ALREADY ONSITE

Any IP camera, any brand, including the ones installed years ago. Nothing gets ripped out, rewired, or replaced to start.

STEP TWO: THE CHeKT AI SERVER

 Twelve channels of deep learning running on NVIDIA Jetson. People, vehicle types, object and bag detection, zones and lines, dwell, direction, tailgating, counting, slip and fall, aggressive behavior.

STEP THREE: CHeKT VIDEO CONTROL PANEL

This is the step nobody else has.This is the step that turns a detection into an alarm. Detection becomes an armed alarm zone with a real schedule and signal, over an outbound-only encrypted connection — no port forwarding, no per-camera setup.

STEP FOUR: THE MONITORING CENTER

The alarm lands inside the automation platform the center already runs, across eighteen-plus integrations. Trained operators, written SOPs, and redundant infrastructure — staffed around the clock, run by your dealers.

STEP FIVE: VERIFIED RESPONSE

The operator views the live clip, issues a talk-down, requests priority dispatch, and files an incident report. That’s an alarm, not a notification.

Wide detection, filtered before it alarms.

A wide detection library and real alarm logic mean operators act on the events that matter — not on every flicker of motion.

  • Precision filtering: Vehicle types, bag detection, dwell, direction, tailgating and counting cut noise before it reaches an operator.
  • Real alarm zones, not tags: Every detection maps to a zone type and arming schedule on the Video Control Panel, so it behaves like a monitored point, not a metadata tag.
  • One box, twelve streams: Twelve channels of analytics run on one appliance instead of twelve.

What Is Tailgating Detection?

Tailgating detection flags when a second person follows an authorized person through an access point without being authorized themselves. The CKTAI612 lists tailgating in its detection library, so the event can reach an operator as an alarm instead of staying in a log.

tWO WAYS TO DEPLOYTwo Ways to Deploy the CKTAI612: Alarm Path or VMS Path

One appliance. Two revenue models.

Sell the AI Server as a monitored alarm point paired with a Video Control Panel, or as a clean analytics feed into a VMS a customer already runs. Either way, the hardware doesn’t change — only how you bill it.

THE ALARM PATH

Pair with a CHeKT Video Control Panel. Every AI detection becomes a monitored video alarm event at the central station, billed monthly like any other alarm point.

  • Recurring monthly revenue per site rather than a one-time hardware sale
  • Interior AI detection can stand in for interior motion in a commercial kit
  • Operator gets clip plus live view, talk-down and dispatch
  • Works with the center the dealer already uses

THE VMS PATH

Run it as an ONVIF AI encoder. Stream analytics metadata and video into an existing VMS or NVR, with no requirement to change the recording platform.

  • ONVIF PullPoint subscription for event delivery
  • Event triggers over ONVIF topics, email, TCP and HTTP
  • Encoder function for NVRs that need a clean stream
  • An entry point that upgrades to the alarm path later
#25NEW: decision guide under the two paths

Which Path Should You Choose?

Choose the Alarm Path when you want to sell the CKTAI612 as a monitored alarm point, with live view, talk-down, and dispatch at a monitoring center. Choose the VMS Path when a customer already runs a VMS or NVR and only needs analytics events and clean video from the AI Server. The VMS Path can also be an entry point that upgrades to the Alarm Path later.

CKTAI612 Frequently Asked Questions

How many cameras can the CKTAI612 analyze?

The CKTAI612 analyzes twelve camera channels on one appliance, running deep learning on twelve simultaneous streams.

Does the CKTAI612 work with any camera brand?

Yes, the CKTAI612 works with existing IP cameras, encoders, and recorders from any manufacturer, including hardware installed years ago.

Is the CHeKT AI Server NDAA and TAA compliant?

Yes, the CKTAI612 is NDAA and TAA compliant and is built on NVIDIA Jetson hardware.

Which detections require the advanced license?

Slip and fall, aggressive behavior, and missing person detection require the advanced license on the CKTAI612.

Can the CKTAI612 send events to a VMS or NVR?

Yes, the CKTAI612 can send events to a VMS or NVR. In the VMS Path it works as an ONVIF AI encoder and streams analytics metadata and video, with events delivered through ONVIF PullPoint subscription, ONVIF topics, email, TCP, and HTTP.

Does the CKTAI612 require port forwarding?

No, the CKTAI612 does not require port forwarding. It uses an encrypted, outbound-only connection, so nothing is exposed on the internet.

How do CKTAI612 detections reach a monitoring center?

In the Alarm Path, the CHeKT Video Control Panel turns each detection into an armed alarm zone. The alarm then lands in the automation platform the monitoring center already runs, across eighteen-plus integrations.
