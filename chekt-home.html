FIND A VIDEO MONITORING PROVIDER

Goodbye passive video systems.
Hello, proactive visual security.

With CHeKT, video isn’t an afterthought — it’s the frontline.

Traditional video systems are reactive by design. Video footage is reviewed after the fact — too late to prevent the crime itself. CHeKT changes the game. 

Our Proactive Visual Security platform puts video at the center of protection. Cameras, alarm panels, IP speakers, and AI analytics work together in the cloud, turning every device into part of a live, coordinated defense. That is remote video monitoring (RVM) done proactively: operators at a monitoring center see live video the moment an alarm triggers, verify what is happening, and can deter intruders and guide police to the scene.

CHeKT’s software verifies alarms in real time, triggering immediate, precise security responses. Operators don’t just watch crime happen — they can stop it before it gets started, with accuracy that helps reduce false alarms and speed every decision.
